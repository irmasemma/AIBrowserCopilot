**Body:**
AgentHub is a Chrome extension that turns your browser into a set of MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools, so your AI can work inside your actual, logged-in browser — no more copy-pasting page content into a chat window.

Install the extension, then run one command in any terminal — `npx agenthub-setup@latest`. The installer auto-detects the AI tools already on your machine (Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop, or any MCP client) and registers a local bridge with each. No manual config files, no account, no sign-up.

Once connected, your AI can list and read open tabs, fill forms (including React, Vue, and shadow-DOM inputs), click, scroll, press keys, navigate, read full page content and metadata, take accessibility snapshots and screenshots, and extract tables and structured data from any page.

It's built privacy-first. The bridge runs on your machine and listens only on localhost (127.0.0.1) — not reachable from your network. AgentHub runs no server that reads or stores your browsing; page data flows directly between your browser and your AI tool. Site access is opt-in and revocable, a built-in blocklist keeps AI off banking and email domains by default, and every tool call is logged in the side panel.

Perfect for research, QA, form-filling, and data extraction. Requires Chrome/Edge 120+ and an MCP-compatible AI client.

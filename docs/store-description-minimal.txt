AI browser automation — use the built-in chat or connect Claude Code, Cursor, and other MCP-compatible AI tools to automate your browser. Control data flow, stay private. Full MCP to your browser.

Two ways to use it:

MCP integration: Connect external AI tools via Model Context Protocol (MCP) to bring AI to any website. Tested with Claude Code, Cursor, and Claude Desktop out of the box — works with any MCP-compatible client. Full list of tested clients on GitHub: github.com/irmasemma/AIBrowserCopilot

Built-in chat: Chat with OpenAI directly in the browser sidebar to automate tasks, read pages, fill forms.

What you can do:
• Read and extract page content
• Fill forms and interact with pages
• Take screenshots
• Automate workflows without leaving your browser

Privacy & control:
• Your OpenAI API key (if using chat) stays local in your browser
• Data goes directly to your chosen AI provider — never through AgentHub servers
• Blocklist protects banking and email sites by default
• Full control over which sites can use AI

Use cases:
• Research: summarize and extract from any website
• Automation: fill forms, test sites, scrape data
• Productivity: get AI help without context-switching
